The work we take off your desk

Most of it is copy, paste, and remind

Someone re-types invoice details into the accounting system. Someone rebuilds the same report every Sunday. Someone chases a signature over WhatsApp, then chases it again. None of it needs a person, and all of it is being done by one.

We find that work and replace it with something that runs on its own. We build it on the tools you already pay for, it takes weeks rather than quarters, and your team can open it and see what it is doing. That last part matters more than it sounds at 4pm on a Thursday when something breaks.

How to start

Evidence

What we have shipped

Field operations

20 crews, run out of one Excel sheet

A field-services company was scheduling 20 crews from a shared spreadsheet. We replaced it with a dispatch system: jobs routed to the right manager, crews scheduled by day, an SLA clock on every job, an audit trail on every change, and the whole interface in Arabic and English.

First working version in under a week. The SLA clock and the audit trail landed in the second.

Our own product

We run the software we sell

OrbitBook is a bilingual booking platform for small businesses, built and operated by VTEC. It handles its own customers, its own emails, and its own uptime.

The consultation you book on this page runs on it. See OrbitBook

Arabic is not a translation layer

Built to survive an Arabic audit

Both systems above run right-to-left Arabic as a first-class language, not a translated skin. Dates, phone numbers and Latin identifiers stay readable inside Arabic text, and every state change is written to a trail a reviewer can follow.

Most automation shops in this region ship English-only. That is the gap we build in.

Where we stop

What we do not do

We do not train models, sell GPU capacity, or run six-month digital transformation programmes. We take AI and automation tools that already work and wire them into your existing systems.
