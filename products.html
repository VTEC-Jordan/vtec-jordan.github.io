Product 01

OrbitBook

Closed Beta

Bookings that orbit your business — arriving, confirmed, dependable.

OrbitBook is a booking platform for small businesses in Jordan and the region. Your customers get a clean booking page in Arabic or English where they pick a time in seconds — no account, no app. You get a full dashboard: calendar, services, working hours, notifications, and reports. Built, hosted, and run entirely by VTEC.

What it does

Everything a booking needs

Bilingual by design

Arabic and English are both first-class: the booking page, the dashboard, emails, and notifications all switch language — with proper right-to-left layout, not an afterthought.

A booking page, not an app

Every business gets its own public page with its logo, colors, and services. Customers book in seconds from any phone — no download, no sign-up.

No double-bookings. Ever.

Availability comes from your real working hours and closures, and the platform makes overlapping bookings impossible at the database level.

A calendar you'll actually use

Day, week, and month views of every booking, with filters and one-click CSV export when you need the numbers somewhere else.

Confirmations that arrive

Customers get confirmation and reminder emails in their own language. You get an alert in your inbox and in the dashboard the moment a booking lands or cancels.

Your brand, day and night

Upload your logo, pick your accent color, and the whole experience follows — including a full light and dark theme across every screen.
